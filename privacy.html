Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Movelle is a small marketing agency run by Pablo Najera. We use a social publishing tool at social.getmovelle.com to schedule and publish posts on Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube for the accounts we manage, including our own. This policy explains what happens to your information when a Facebook Page, an Instagram account, or a YouTube channel is connected to that tool.

What we collect

  • From Facebook and Instagram (through Meta): your name, profile picture, and Facebook ID; the businesses, Pages, and Instagram professional accounts you manage, with their names, usernames, IDs, and pictures, so you can choose which ones to connect; access tokens for your Facebook account and for the Pages and Instagram accounts you manage, which the tool uses to list them for you and to publish to the ones you connect; and statistics for the Pages and accounts you connect and their posts, such as reach, likes, comments, and follower counts.
  • From YouTube (through Google): your Google account's name, email address, and profile picture; your channel's ID, name, web address, and picture; access and refresh tokens that let the tool upload and manage videos on that channel; and statistics for the channel and its videos, such as views, likes, watch time, and subscriber counts.
  • Content to publish: the text, images, and videos for the posts we schedule.
  • Cookies: the tool sets a sign-in cookie, and Cloudflare, which protects the tool, sets its own sign-in cookies. They are only set on the devices of people who sign in to the tool.

How we use it

Only to publish the posts that you, or Movelle at your request, schedule, and to show how your posts and accounts are performing. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build profiles about you, and we do not use it for anything else.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only with:

  • Meta and Google, to publish your posts on Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube and to get their statistics.
  • Cloudflare, which carries traffic to and from the tool and handles its sign-in.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Where it is stored

On servers that Movelle operates. Only Movelle can sign in to the tool, and access tokens are used only by the tool to do the work described above. Images and videos uploaded for a post are stored at a web address that Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube download them from when the post goes out, so anyone with that exact address could open the file.

How long we keep it

For as long as the account stays connected. When you ask us, we permanently delete the access tokens and everything we hold from that account, including copies of uploaded media, within 7 days. If you remove our access to a YouTube channel, we also delete that channel's data within 7 days, as Google requires.

How to remove access and delete your data

You can remove Movelle's access at any time:

  • Facebook and Instagram: in Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations (or Apps and websites), and remove Movelle. Instagram access comes through Facebook, so this removes it for Instagram too.
  • YouTube: go to Google's security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions and remove Movelle.

To have us delete what we hold, email pablo@najera.co with "Delete my data" in the subject and the name of the account. We will permanently delete it within 7 days.

Deleting the data Movelle holds does not delete anything on Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube. Posts and videos that were already published stay there until you delete them on those platforms.

YouTube

Movelle uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the YouTube channels connected to the tool. Google's use of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Terms of use: by connecting a YouTube channel to the tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or complaints about this policy or how we handle your information: pablo@najera.co.
